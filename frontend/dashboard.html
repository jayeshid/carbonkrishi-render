What you can do

From a single field plan to a portfolio report.

🧪

Run scenarios

Compare a baseline against any number of "what-if" plans side-by-side. Diff cards highlight gains and trade-offs across all 18 impact categories.

📥

Export results

Download the full LCA result as CSV, JSON, or a print-ready PDF brief — suitable for co-operative reports, audit trails, or carbon-credit submissions.

🌐

Switch languages

Every numerical inference is generated in English, Hindi, and Telugu. The toggle is live — translations appear without a page reload.

🔍

Trace every number

Click any value to see its provenance: source factor, OpenLCA flow ID, IPCC tier, and the exact regression coefficient that produced it.

Built for the field

Designed for daily decisions, not quarterly reports.

The dashboard runs entirely in the browser — no signup, no installs. Every interaction is instantaneous, every output is auditable.

⚡ Instant feedback

Every slider, dropdown, and input is wired to the model. Predictions, charts, and carbon-credit estimates refresh in under 50 ms — fast enough for an extension worker to tune a plan with the farmer in the room.

🎯 ISO-grade rigour

The pipeline implements ISO 14040 / 14044 phases end-to-end: goal & scope, life-cycle inventory, impact assessment (ReCiPe 2016 H), and interpretation. Hand-off to a certified practitioner is one CSV away.

📱 Mobile-friendly

The layout adapts down to a 360 px viewport — usable on a typical field-worker smartphone. Charts collapse to scrollable strips; sliders become large tap targets.

🔓 No black-boxes

Source factors, model coefficients, and emission factors are all exposed under Model Info. You can replicate any number with a calculator and the citations provided.

Getting started

Three steps to your first LCA.

  1. 1

    Describe the field

    Crop, area, water regime, soil class. Sensible defaults pre-fill from typical Telangana paddy systems.

  2. 2

    Set the fertiliser plan

    Pick urea, DAP, MOP, FYM, vermicompost, biofertiliser doses — or load a preset blend.

  3. 3

    Read the results

    Open any module from the sidebar. Tweak inputs, compare scenarios, export the report.

  4. 4

    Act on it

    Use the inference summary to brief the farmer; submit the credit estimate to your co-operative's CCTS aggregator.
