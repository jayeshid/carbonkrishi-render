Home / Methodology

Science-grade accounting, simple inputs.

We combine internationally-recognised LCA methods with locally-calibrated agronomic data so every kilogram of fertiliser maps to a credible environmental cost.

Standards we follow

Four pillars of credibility.

Every prediction is anchored in a peer-reviewed framework so results are auditable, comparable, and defensible.

🔬

ISO-aligned LCA

Cradle-to-field system boundary covering upstream production, transport, and on-field emissions in line with ISO 14040 / 14044. Allocation, system boundary, and impact-assessment choices are all documented.

🌡️

IPCC 2019 factors

Direct & indirect N₂O, CH₄ from rice paddies, NO₃⁻ leaching and NH₃ volatilisation — using the IPCC 2019 Refinement national tier-1 / tier-2 emission factors for tropical irrigated rice.

🧪

SALCA losses

Phosphorus runoff and soil-eutrophication factors from the Swiss SALCA framework, adapted to Indian alluvial and red-soil conditions through ICAR-IIRR field sampling.

🌍

ReCiPe 2016 (H)

Mid-point characterisation across Climate Change (kg CO₂-eq), Eutrophication (kg P-eq), Acidification (kg SO₂-eq), and Ecotoxicity (CTUe) — using the hierarchical (H) perspective.

Under the hood

Ridge regression on 13,042 OpenLCA records.

Two separate models — one for conventional, one for organic — each trained on parametric OpenLCA runs validated against field observations.

🤖

Algorithm

Multivariate Ridge regression (L2-regularised) — chosen for stability, interpretability, and well-behaved extrapolation across input ranges.

📚

Training data

7,116 conventional + 5,926 organic LCA records, generated in OpenLCA via parametric sampling across realistic input ranges for Indian rice.

🌾

Primary data

Field measurements from ICAR-IIRR Hyderabad and KVK Medak, Telangana were used to validate model outputs and tune local emission factors.

📈

Validation

Hold-out R² > 0.85 across all four impact categories. Validation plots are available in the Model Info tab of the dashboard.

System boundary

What's included in each prediction.

🏭

Upstream production

Manufacturing of urea, DAP, MOP, zinc sulphate, FYM & compost, including energy and feedstock impacts.

🚛

Transport

Average inland transport distances from manufacturing hubs to the farm gate within India.

🌾

Field emissions

CH₄ from flooded paddies, N₂O direct + indirect, NH₃ volatilisation, NO₃⁻ leaching, PO₄³⁻ runoff.

⚖️

Functional unit

Per hectare per cropping season — the natural unit for farm-level decisions.
