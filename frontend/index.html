📚 What this app does

Rice fertiliser application drives ~3,000–7,000 kg CO₂-eq/ha/season via upstream production and field emissions. CarbonKrishi uses Ridge regression models trained on 13,042 OpenLCA records to predict the full impact of any input mix and suggest farmer-friendly mitigations in 3 languages.

Primary data: ICAR-IIRR Hyderabad & KVK Medak, Telangana.

🚀 Get started

  1. Open Predictor and enter your N/P/K/Zn or FYM/Compost rates.
  2. Use Gradient to find the cost-optimal organic transition.
  3. Estimate revenue in Credits from soil-carbon storage.
  4. Audit field-level losses in Emissions.

ℹ️ About CarbonKrishi AI & Model Methodology

Algorithm: Ridge Regression (multivariate, L2-regularised) — trained separately for conventional and organic rice systems.

System Boundary: Cradle-to-field — upstream production, transport, and field-level emissions (N₂O, NO₃⁻, NH₃, PO₄³⁻) via IPCC and SALCA methodologies.

Training Data: Conventional 7,116 LCA records + Organic 5,926 LCA records (OpenLCA, parametric sampling).

Primary Data: ICAR-IIRR Hyderabad and KVK Medak, Telangana.

Impact Assessment: ReCiPe 2016 Midpoint (H).

Cost References: Indian market rates for fertilisers (2024) and organic amendments.

Features:

  • ✅ Single prediction for conventional or organic systems
  • ✅ Blend analysis: explore transition from conventional to organic
  • ✅ Cost-benefit analysis with Indian market rates
  • ✅ Environmental impact quantification (GWP, Eutrophication, Acidification, Ecotoxicity)
  • ✅ Validation plots and model statistics
  • ✅ Recommendations based on agronomic best practices
