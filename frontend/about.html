Mission

Big science, small footprint.

Two convictions guide the project.

🎯 Our Mission

Make ISO-grade Life-Cycle Assessment as accessible to a smallholder rice farmer as it is to a multinational sustainability team — without diluting the science. We pair Ridge regression models trained on 13,042 OpenLCA records with primary field data from ICAR-IIRR Hyderabad and KVK Medak, Telangana.

🌐 Why it matters

Rice paddies generate roughly 10% of global agricultural greenhouse-gas emissions. India alone cultivates ~44 million ha of rice. Optimising fertiliser plans on even a fraction of these fields can avoid millions of tonnes of CO₂-eq per year while improving farmer income through the CCTS soil-carbon market.

What we stand for

Three principles guide the build.

🔬

Rigour

Every number traceable to a published factor, model, or field measurement. No black-box magic.

🤝

Accessibility

Three languages, mobile-friendly UI, no signup. The science should meet farmers where they are.

🌱

Action
